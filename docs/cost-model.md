# Cost model

> Generated by `scripts/cost_model.py` from the stored run (`evals/archive/20261009/`), the model prices in `config/models.yaml` and the AWS prices in `docs/cost-model.prices.json` (read from the AWS Price List on 2026-10-09). Do not edit by hand: change the inputs and rebuild it.

A question costs the model tokens plus a few cents of plumbing, and a month costs a fixed bill for the stack plus the questions. Per-question figures are the mean of the 80 golden cases of the run, a mix of 55 answerable questions, 15 ambiguous or out of scope and 10 write requests; a different mix costs differently.

## What stands out

- **Fixed or variable depends on volume.** For `bedrock-claude` the always-on stack is 33% of the $126.51 a month at 100 questions a day ($0.042 a question all in, against $0.028 of variable cost) and 0.5% at 10,000 a day, where the model is the cost.
- **The largest fixed item is 3 interface endpoints (24/7) ($21.90 a month).** They let a function in the VPC reach Bedrock and Secrets Manager without a NAT gateway (ADR-014).
- **Metrics were a cost bug, found by this model and fixed.** The first design repeated every metric for each of the 4 values of `Status`: 55 series a profile, $16.50 a month, which would have been the second largest fixed item, for data the dashboard never read. Now each metric is by profile and only `Questions` is by status: 15 series, $4.50 a month. The stack that was deployed for the measurements (33 series) still had the first design; it was destroyed before the fix.
- **Capacity.** With 2 workers `bedrock-claude` carries 14,401 questions a day and `bedrock-claude-opus` 7,450; at 10,000 a day the reserved concurrency has to go up (`-c reserved_concurrency=N`), which costs nothing by itself on Lambda. A single local model carries 1,233 a day.

## Assumptions

- The mean latency of the run is the time a worker is busy; a client polls every 3 s.
- A result is about 20 KB in DynamoDB: 22 write units and 5 read units a poll.
- The guardrail is called 4 times a question (the question; the answer and its warnings and assumptions), one text unit each, with the content, topic and sensitive-information policies.
- 4 KB of logs a question; 4 bytes a token through the endpoints.
- Lambda, API Gateway and CloudWatch stay in their first price tier at these volumes.

## Not included

Data transfer out, KMS, a support plan, taxes, Transaction Search ingestion (nothing without traffic; spans bill as logs with it), the AWS free tier, Bedrock quotas and throttling, and any saving from prompt caching or batch inference, neither of which was measured. The golden set is a draft, so the *answers* these costs buy are provisional even if the costs are not.
